<h1 class="font-bold text-2xl mb-2">↩️ RETURNS & REFUNDS POLICY — LifeStyleQ8</h1>

<h1 class="font-bold text-xl mt-6 mb-2">1. Overview</h1>
<p class="mt-0">
At LifeStyleQ8, customer satisfaction is our priority. This policy explains when you can request 
a return, replacement, or refund.
</p>

<h1 class="font-bold text-xl mt-6 mb-2">2. Return Eligibility</h1>
<p>You may request a return under the following conditions:</p>

<ul class="list-none ml-6 mt-3">
    <li>✔ <b>Wrong item received</b><br>
        <span class="text-gray-700">(Item does not match the order placed)</span>
    </li>

    <li>✔ <b>Defective item out of the box</b><br>
        Dead-on-arrival (DOA) items must be reported within <b>24–48 hours</b>.
    </li>

    <li>✔ <b>Item not as described</b><br>
        Provided the item is unused and in original packaging.
    </li>

    <li>✔ <b>Accessories or small items</b><br>
        Must be unopened and unused.
    </li>
</ul>

<h1 class="font-bold text-xl mt-6 mb-2">3. Conditions for Returns</h1>
<p>A return will <b>NOT</b> be accepted if:</p>

<ul class="list-disc ml-4 mt-3">
    <li>Product is used or shows signs of wear</li>
    <li>Original packaging is missing</li>
    <li>Accessories/manuals are missing</li>
    <li>Item is damaged by the customer</li>
    <li>Screen protectors, cases, or hygiene items have been opened</li>
    <li>Software has been modified</li>
    <li>iCloud/Google account is not removed</li>
</ul>

<h1 class="font-bold text-xl mt-6 mb-2">4. Refund Options</h1>
<p>After we receive and inspect the item, one of the following may be applied:</p>

<ul class="list-none p-0 ml-4 mt-2 mb-2">
    <li>✔ <b>Full Refund</b> (same payment method)</li>
    <li>✔ <b>Store Credit</b></li>
    <li>✔ <b>Replacement Product</b></li>
</ul>

<p><b>Refund processing time:</b> 3–10 business days (depending on bank/payment provider).</p>

<h1 class="font-bold text-xl mt-6 mb-2">5. Non-Refundable Items</h1>
<p>The following items are <b>not eligible</b> for refund:</p>

<ul class="list-disc ml-4 mt-2 mb-2">
    <li>Opened earbuds or hygiene products</li>
    <li>Installed screen protectors</li>
    <li>Used accessories</li>
    <li>Digital products or software</li>
    <li>Items purchased during clearance/flash sales</li>
    <li>Repair services (service fees are non-refundable)</li>
</ul>

<h1 class="font-bold text-xl mt-6 mb-2">6. Return Process</h1>
<p>To request a return:</p>

<ul class="list-disc ml-4 mt-2 mb-2">
    <li>Contact LifeStyleQ8 via WhatsApp or email</li>
    <li>Provide order number & product details</li>
    <li>Share photos/videos showing the issue</li>
    <li>Our team will review and approve/reject the request</li>
    <li>If approved, ship or bring the item to our location</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Inspection time:</b> 1–3 business days</p>

<h1 class="font-bold text-xl mt-6 mb-2">7. Shipping for Returns</h1>

<ul class="list-disc ml-4">
    <li>Return shipping cost is the customer’s responsibility unless the item is defective or incorrect.</li>
    <li>If the return is approved and the product is defective, LifeStyleQ8 will cover return shipping.</li>
</ul>

<h1 class="font-bold text-xl mt-6 mb-2">8. Cancellation Policy</h1>

<p>
Orders can be canceled only if they have <b>NOT</b> been shipped or prepared.
