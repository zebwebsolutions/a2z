<h1 class="font-bold text-xl mt-6 mb-2">1. Overview</h1>
<p class="mt-0">
At A2Z, we are committed to providing high-quality products and reliable customer support.
All products purchased from A2Z come with a warranty as specified by the manufacturer or our store warranty conditions.
</p>
<p class="mt-0">
This policy explains what is covered, what is not covered, and how customers can claim warranty service.
</p>

<h1 class="font-bold text-xl mt-6 mb-2">2. Warranty Duration</h1>
<p>Warranty duration varies depending on the item:</p>

<p><b>Mobile Phones & Tablets</b><br>12 months, unless stated otherwise.</p>
<p><b>Laptops</b><br>12–24 months depending on the model.</p>
<p><b>Smart Watches & Wearables</b><br>6–12 months.</p>
<p><b>Earbuds & Audio Accessories</b><br>6 months.</p>
<p><b>Chargers, Cables, Power Banks & Basic Accessories</b><br>30 days replacement warranty.</p>
<p><b>Refurbished / Open-Box Items</b><br>Warranty varies by item and will be clearly mentioned on the product page.</p>

<h1 class="font-bold text-xl mt-6 mb-2">3. What is Covered</h1>
<p>Warranty covers the following:</p>

<ul class="list-disc ml-12">
    <li>Manufacturing defects</li>
    <li>Internal component failure</li>
    <li>Battery defects (as per manufacturer terms)</li>
    <li>Software malfunction due to factory issues</li>
    <li>Touchscreen malfunction (non physical damage)</li>
    <li>Speaker, microphone, and charging IC issues not caused by misuse</li>
</ul>

<h1 class="font-bold text-xl">4. What is NOT Covered</h1>
<p>Warranty does not cover:</p>

<ul class="list-disc ml-12">
    <li>Physical damage (cracks, dents, bending, chips, scratches)</li>
    <li>Water or liquid damage (unless IP-rated and approved after inspection)</li>
    <li>Damage caused by misuse, drops, pressure, or unauthorized repair</li>
    <li>Dead pixels within manufacturer tolerance limits</li>
    <li>Burn-in or retention on OLED displays</li>
    <li>Accessories such as cables, cases, screen protectors</li>
    <li>Battery wear due to normal usage</li>
    <li>Software modification, rooting, or jailbreaking</li>
</ul>

<h1 class="font-bold text-xl mt-6 mb-2">5. Warranty for Repair Services (A2Z Repair Center)</h1>

<p>We offer:</p>
<ul class="list-disc ml-12">
    <li>30-day warranty on repair services</li>
    <li>7-day warranty on aftermarket parts</li>
    <li>90-day warranty on premium/original parts (if used)</li>
</ul>

<p>Warranty does not apply if:</p>
<ul class="list-disc ml-12">
    <li>The device is opened by another technician</li>
    <li>Additional damage occurs after repair</li>
    <li>The device suffers liquid damage</li>
    <li>The customer declines recommended repairs</li>
</ul>

<h1 class="font-bold text-xl mt-6 mb-2">6. Warranty Claim Process</h1>
<p>To file a warranty claim:</p>

<ul class="list-disc ml-12">
    <li>Contact us via WhatsApp or email</li>
    <li>Provide the order number and proof of purchase</li>
    <li>Describe the issue in detail</li>
    <li>Bring or ship the device to our service center</li>
    <li>Our team will inspect and approve/reject the claim</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Inspection time:</b> 1–5 business days</p>

<p>If the warranty is approved:</p>
<ul class="list-disc ml-12">
    <li>✔ Device will be repaired free of charge</li>
    <li>✔ Replacement may be provided (if repair is not possible)</li>
</ul>

<p>If the warranty is rejected:</p>
<ul class="list-disc ml-12">
    <li>✖ A detailed explanation will be provided</li>
    <li>✖ Paid repair options will be offered</li>
</ul>

<h1 class="font-bold text-xl mt-6 mb-2">7. Manufacturer Warranty</h1>
<p>
For products sold with official manufacturer warranty, customers may contact the authorized service center directly.
</p>

<h1 class="font-bold text-xl mt-6 mb-2">8. Shipping for Warranty Claims</h1>

<p>
Shipping to our service center is the customer's responsibility.
Return shipping will be covered by A2Z only if the warranty claim is approved.
</p>
